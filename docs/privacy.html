Privacy Policy

PillBuddy · Last updated: November 17, 2026

PillBuddy (“the App”) is a medication reminder application. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App stores, how it is used, and your choices. By using PillBuddy, you agree to this policy.

1. Who we are

PillBuddy is provided by Techlad (“we”, “us”). The App is designed to help you organize medication reminders on your own device. For privacy questions, contact us at privacy@techlad.com (replace with your real contact email before publishing).

2. Information we collect

PillBuddy stores data locally on your device only. We do not require an account, and the App does not send your medication or personal data to our servers.

Depending on how you use the App, it may store:

  • A first name or display name you enter in settings
  • Medication or reminder names, schedules, and related notes you enter
  • Dose history (for example, taken, missed, or snoozed events)
  • App preferences such as time format, notification timing, snooze length, text size, contrast, haptics, and spoken confirmation
  • Technical flags used to schedule and deliver reminders on your device

We do not intentionally collect government IDs, payment information, precise location, contacts, photos, or health records from other apps.

3. How information is used

Local data is used only to:

  • Show your reminders and daily progress
  • Schedule and deliver notifications and alarms
  • Apply your accessibility and preference settings
  • Restore your reminders and settings if Android Auto Backup or device-to-device transfer is available and enabled on your device

4. Permissions

PillBuddy may request or use the following Android permissions:

  • Notifications — to alert you when a dose is due
  • Exact alarms — to fire reminders at the times you set
  • Boot completed — to reschedule reminders after reboot or app update
  • Vibrate — for optional haptic feedback

You can revoke notification and related permissions in your device settings. Doing so may prevent reminders from working as expected.

5. Sharing and third parties

We do not sell your personal information. Because medication and settings data stay on your device, we do not share that content with advertisers or analytics providers through the App itself.

Your device manufacturer, Google (including Google Play and Android backup services, if enabled), and your network operator may process device-level information under their own policies when you install or use the App.

6. Data retention and deletion

Data remains on your device until you delete it in the App (for example, removing a reminder) or uninstall PillBuddy. Uninstalling the App removes local App data, except where Android backup has retained a copy that may restore on reinstall according to your device settings.

7. Children’s privacy

PillBuddy is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information in the App on a shared device, delete the App data or uninstall the App.

8. International users

Data is stored on the device where the App is installed. If you travel or transfer a device, that local data travels with the device or with any backup you choose to use.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.

10. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy: privacy@techlad.com
